FAQ

What is stockfable AI Studio?
stockfable AI Studio is a collection of AI-powered editing tools that you can use before licensing and downloading content. It lets you search our catalog of premium content and then edit and download professional-quality images and videos in one place. Adjust colors and moods, change image ratios to fit specific placements, swap backgrounds, animate stills, and more — all without leaving stockfable.

We also know that what you see today is a starting point. Guided by the feedback of our customers, stockfable AI Studio will continue to become more capable, provide more precision, and offer more support for your evolving creative workflow.
Do I have to use stockfable AI Studio?
No, it's completely optional. You can browse, license, and download content without ever using the AI Studio tools.
Do I need a stockfable subscription?
A free account lets you browse and preview. A subscription unlocks unlimited downloads, AI Studio features, and commercial licensing.
How do I access stockfable AI Studio?
Sign in to your stockfable account and click the "AI Studio" button on any image or video detail page.
Can I edit content before I license it?
Yes. You can preview and edit any content in AI Studio before purchasing a license.
Can I upload my own images or video to edit?
Yes, premium subscribers can upload their own media to edit using AI Studio tools.
How do I get generative credits, and do they roll over?
Generative credits are included with your subscription each month. Unused credits roll over for up to 90 days.
How can I determine whether content I edit with AI Studio is commercially safe to use?
All AI Studio content comes with a commercial license. Check the license details on each asset's page for specific usage rights.
What AI models are used in stockfable AI Studio?
We use a combination of proprietary in-house models and industry-leading third-party models, all optimized for creative workflows.
